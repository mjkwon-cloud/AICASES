10 — FAQ

Before You Buy

Does it leave a sticky residue?

No. The lightweight essence absorbs cleanly into the skin without a sticky finish.

Can I use one every day?

Yes — the sheets are gentle enough for a daily "one mask, every use" hydration routine.

How long should I leave it on?

15 to 20 minutes. Don't let the sheet dry out completely on your face.

Is it safe for very sensitive skin?

It suits all skin types from oily to sensitive, but we always recommend a patch test first.

Can I reuse a sheet if essence is left in the pouch?

No — the cotton sheet is single use. Apply the leftover essence to your neck, chest and arms.

What is the shelf life unopened?

Generally up to 36 months from manufacture* — always check the date printed on the pouch.
